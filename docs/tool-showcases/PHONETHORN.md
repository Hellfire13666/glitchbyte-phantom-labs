## What it does

PHONETHORN is a separate, beginner-oriented Python phone intelligence and recovery project. Its current development scope includes:

- Offline phone-number formatting and region/type hints through `phonenumbers`
- Public business-contact research links (Web Echo) and approved public-page checks
- Optional authenticated carrier lookup through an external paid provider
- Approximate IP geolocation, coordinate map links and local photograph GPS metadata
- Official lost-Android/iPhone recovery instructions and connected-device diagnostics
- Fusion reporting with provenance and HTML/JSON/TXT evidence exports

**Limits:** An ordinary phone number does not reveal live handset GPS or private subscriber records. Phone area codes and IP geolocation are not current-device location proof. Use the tool for authorized research, public organizational information and device recovery.
